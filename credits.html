Data sources and credits

  • Card data and images: card names, numbers, images and set information come from public catalogue data. They belong to their owners (Bandai and the One Piece Card Game) and are used to identify cards.
  • Prices: TCGplayer market prices, collected via TCGCSV. Additional price data from JustTCG for cards in your collection.
  • Exchange rates: daily rates from a public exchange-rate source, shown for convenience only.
  • Card recognition: DINOv2 model by Meta (Apache-2.0 licence), run on your phone with Microsoft ONNX Runtime (MIT licence). Card text is read with Google ML Kit on the device.
  • App framework and libraries: Flutter, Supabase and other open-source packages. See Open-source licences in the app for the full list.

SleeveX is an unofficial fan app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Bandai or the One Piece Card Game.
