Delete your SleeveX account and data

SleeveX is a trading name of TJXLabs, a company in Sydney, Australia ("we", "us"). This page is for the SleeveX app on Google Play.

Delete it in the app (fastest)

  1. Open SleeveX and go to the Account tab.
  2. Tap Settings (the gear).
  3. In the Account section tap Delete account and cloud data. If you never saved an account with an email, the button says Delete my data.
  4. Read the message and tap Delete. It cannot be undone.

The deletion happens straight away. You will be signed out.

Cannot use the app? Ask us by email

Email tjxlabs@gmail.com from the address on your account, with the subject "Delete my SleeveX account", and give your SleeveX username if you have one. We will reply within 30 days, and delete your account and data as described below.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, username, bio and profile photo.
  • The online backup of your collection, wishlist and binders, and your collector page and showcase.
  • Your posts, comments, threads and replies, and their photos. Threads you started are removed with your account.
  • Your follows, blocks, likes, deals, price alerts, notification choices and notification address.
  • Your sign-in details (email address and password hash) and your Pro subscription record on our servers.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Your collection on your phone. For an account with an email, the cards stay on your phone. Delete my data (no email) also clears the phone.
  • Reports you made about other people stay, with no link to you.
  • The moderation log (actions by our staff, with the reason) is kept for up to one year. It names the staff member, not you as an ordinary user.
  • Backups and technical logs held by our hosting provider are overwritten after a limited time.
  • Payment records. Purchases are made through Google Play, which keeps its own records under its own policy. Deleting your SleeveX account does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions) so you are not charged again.

More detail is in our privacy policy.
