Privacy policy

Last updated 25 October 2026. Draft: to be reviewed before the app is published.

The short version

SleeveX keeps your collection on your phone. If you create an account, a backup of it is stored online so you can use it on another device, and you can choose to share a showcase, follow other collectors and post. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking.

What we collect

  • Account: if you sign up, your email address and a secure sign-in token (your password is stored only as a salted hash, never as text). Guests have no account and nothing is sent. If sign-in with another provider such as Google is added later, we will receive the email address that provider shares and will update this policy first.
  • Your collection: the cards you add, quantities, conditions, wishlist and binders. Stored on your phone; copied to our database only when you are signed in.
  • Profile: your username, display name, bio, avatar colour and, if you add one, a profile photo. They are stored in your account. Your username, display name, bio and profile photo are visible to other collectors when you share a showcase, post or are listed in Collectors.
  • Collector page (optional, off until you turn it on): in Account, What collectors can see, you can choose to be listed in Collectors, and to share which cards you own, your portfolio value and graph, and your wishlist. When a switch is on, a copy of just that information (card ids, wishlist ids, total value and its history) is stored in your account for others to see. We never show quantities or what you paid. Turning a switch off hides it straight away.
  • Showcase (optional): up to 12 cards you choose to show, a title, and whether prices are shown. A showcase is private until you set it to "Anyone with the link" or "Listed in Explore". Listed showcases can be found by other signed-in and signed-out visitors.
  • Following and blocking: who you follow and who you block. Other people can see follower counts; your block list is private.
  • Posts, likes and comments: text up to 280 characters, the cards you attach, one optional photo per post, and your likes. Posts, photos and comments are public and show your username and profile photo. Photos are shrunk on your phone before they are uploaded. You can delete your own posts (which removes the photo) and comments.
  • Reports: if you report a profile, showcase, post or comment, we store the report and your reason so we can review it. If you later delete your account the report stays but is no longer linked to you.
  • Price alerts: the cards you set alerts on and your target prices.
  • Notification address: if you allow notifications, a token that identifies your phone so we can send you a price alert. See "Notifications" below.
  • Terms and age: the time you agreed to the Terms to post. When you create an account you confirm you are 13 or older; we do not collect your date of birth.
  • Photos you choose: for a profile photo or a post you pick a photo from your phone's photo picker or take one with the camera. Only the photo you choose is sent, nothing else from your gallery. Please do not upload photos of other people.
  • Card photos: scanning happens on your phone. The camera picture is used to recognise the card on the device and is not uploaded. If a future version offers cloud scanning, a photo is sent only after you choose it, and we will update this policy first.
  • Technical data: our hosting provider may log standard request information (such as IP address and time) to keep the service running and secure.

Notifications

Price-alert notifications are delivered through Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging. If you allow notifications, Google receives your phone's notification token and the text of the alert (the card name and price) so it can reach your phone. Nothing else about you is sent. You can turn notifications off in your phone's settings, and remove an alert at any time in Portfolio, Alerts.

Who handles your data

We do not sell your data or share it for advertising. We use a small number of services to run the app: Supabase (database, sign-in and photo storage), Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (price-alert notifications) and GitHub Pages (this website). Card and price data comes from public sources (TCGCSV and JustTCG) and exchange rates from a public source; those requests carry card identifiers, not your personal details. Links to sellers such as eBay or TCGplayer open on their own sites, which have their own policies.

Where it is stored

Account data is stored with Supabase in the Asia-Pacific (Sydney) region. Card and price data is public catalogue data and contains nothing about you.

Your choices

  • Use SleeveX as a guest: no account, nothing leaves your phone.
  • Delete your account and cloud data any time in Account, Delete account and cloud data. This removes your profile and profile photo, collector page copy, showcase, follows, blocks, posts and post photos, comments, likes, alerts and notification address. Your collection stays on your phone.
  • Keep your showcase private, or turn it off, in Account, My showcase.
  • Ask us to delete or export anything we hold about you by emailing us.

How long we keep it

We keep account data until you delete your account, then remove it from our database. Reports you made stay, with no link to you. Our hosting provider may keep routine technical logs for a short time and backups for a limited period before they are overwritten.

Children

SleeveX is not directed at children under 13. Creating an account requires you to confirm you are 13 or older, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has made an account, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
