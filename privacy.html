Privacy policy

Last updated 25 October 2026. Draft: to be reviewed before the app is published.

The short version

SleeveX keeps your collection on your phone. If you create an account, a backup of it is stored online so you can use it on another device. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking.

What we collect

  • Account: if you sign up, your email address (or the Google account email if you use Google sign-in) and a secure sign-in token. Guests have no account and nothing is sent.
  • Your collection: the cards you add, quantities, conditions, wishlist and binders. Stored on your phone; copied to our database only when you are signed in.
  • Profile: display name, bio and avatar choice. Stored on your phone.
  • Card photos: scanning happens on your phone. The camera picture is used to recognise the card on the device and is not uploaded. If a future version offers cloud scanning, a photo is sent only after you choose it, and we will update this policy first.
  • Technical data: our hosting provider may log standard request information (such as IP address and time) to keep the service running and secure.

Where it is stored

Account data is stored with Supabase in the Asia-Pacific (Sydney) region. Card and price data is public catalogue data and contains nothing about you.

Your choices

  • Use SleeveX as a guest: no account, nothing leaves your phone.
  • Delete your account and cloud data any time in Account, Delete account and cloud data. Your collection stays on your phone.
  • Ask us to delete or export anything we hold about you by emailing us.

Children

SleeveX is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
